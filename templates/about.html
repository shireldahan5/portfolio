About

I like building things for people I can picture using them.

I’m Shirel, a second-year Software Engineering student at McGill, based in Montréal.

(i) Other people’s kids

Most of my jobs so far have involved other people’s kids. I taught swimming and lifeguarded at the Côte Saint-Luc pool, and now I run an after-school study hall for elementary students. So when I built a full-stack app of my own, it was a booking site for my swim lessons, designed around the parents who would actually use it.

(ii) The details

I care about the things people only notice when they’re wrong: spacing that’s slightly off, text that’s hard to read on a phone, a page that jumps around while it loads. This site is where I practise getting them right, mostly in plain HTML and CSS.

(iii) Looking after people

Written down, a lot of what I do outside class comes back to looking after people. I volunteered as a first responder with Magen David Adom, I help out with Chabad Care, and I run Save A Child’s Heart McGill. POP sits right in the middle of that: a two-person startup that turns patients’ medical instructions into care plans, where I’m planning a feature for people living with long-term conditions.

I speak English, French and a little Hebrew. The formal version, with dates, is on the Experience page.
