Who it is for

I have taught private swim lessons to children in Côte Saint-Luc since 2020, from babies at six months to twelve-year-olds working on their strokes. The people using this site are their parents.

Many of the families I teach chose private lessons because they value privacy, modesty and a calm setting. So the copy leads with that: one-on-one lessons, a quiet private pool, no crowds. The rest of the site answers the practical questions: price, policies, and when there is space.

What it does

  • Parents browse open times on an availability calendar.
  • Booking is a request, not an instant reservation. The form is split into five short steps (you, your children, lesson type, schedule, review), and I confirm within 24 hours by email.
  • A password-protected admin page where I manage availability, bookings and reviews.
  • A season notice at the top of the home page, so the site can stay up between summers without people trying to book.

Design decisions

Type. A heavy, soft serif (Fraunces) for the big “swim.” and the headings, and DM Sans for everything else. I wanted it to feel friendly to parents without looking like a children’s site.

Colour. Navy, a pool blue and a warm cream background. The one photo on the home page is of the water itself, not of children, which keeps families’ privacy intact.

Layout. Text and buttons first, photo second. On a phone, the photo moves below the “Book a Lesson” button so the main action is on the first screen.

About the stack

This project uses Next.js, React and Tailwind CSS with a small SQLite database, which is a different setup from this portfolio (plain HTML, CSS and Flask). It is deployed on Railway.
